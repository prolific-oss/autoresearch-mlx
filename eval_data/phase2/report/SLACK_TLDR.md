# Phase 2 Slack TL;DR

Phase 2 report's up: https://storage.googleapis.com/autoresearch-demo-report/index.html

The headline: an AI agent searching autonomously for better training recipes couldn't beat the untrained starting model on its own — a researcher prompting it to look at gaps was the unlock for the only recipes that did improve. The agent's success measure and humans on Prolific broadly agreed on which of those were better, but disagreed on the actual top pick. Two human-in-the-loop moments, both moved the result.
